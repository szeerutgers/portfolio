Complementary

Colors directly opposite on the color wheel. High contrast, vibrant pairings that create visual tension.

Analogous

Adjacent hues on the color wheel. Harmonious and pleasing.

Triadic

Three colors equally spaced on the color wheel. Bold, balanced, and versatile with a rich visual dynamic.
